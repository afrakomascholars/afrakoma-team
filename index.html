The programme in one minute

Afrakoma Scholarships helps promising students start Senior High School without entry costs holding them back.

7 students
2026 SHS Form 1 pilot cohort
GHS 700.00
Each, paid by Mobile Money
3 phases
Application, review, selection

The grant covers books, uniforms, provisions and boarding supplies. Need comes first, with merit as the foundation. Every family is treated with dignity, and every applicant's data is protected.

How this site works

  1. Choose your role

    The Program Director has told you which role you are on. Pick it below.

  2. Do the training

    Read your role page and tick the checklist as you go. It takes about 15 minutes.

  3. Take the pledge

    Enter your email and the personal access code the Program Director gave you, and pledge to respect applicants' data and act with integrity.

  4. Go to your tasks

    Your pledge unlocks the links and tools for your role. Everything you do is recorded with your name.
