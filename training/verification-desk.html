What you see, and what you never do

You are the only team that sees identities and documents, and the only team that can link an Applicant ID back to a real student.

You see

The form responses, the uploads, and the ID key that links each Applicant ID to a name.

The Panel never sees

Names, schools, towns, phone numbers or uploads. You make sure of that.

You never

Score applications, contact families unless the Program Director asks, or copy anything outside the Afrakoma account.

Six rules that keep documents safe

Applicant documents stay inside the afrakomascholars@gmail.com Google account. They never leave it.

Named access only

The Program Director shares folders with your own Google account by name. Link sharing stays off.

2-Step Verification on

Turned on for your account before you get access.

View in place

Open documents in Drive. No downloads, prints, screenshots, photos or forwarding.

No messaging apps

No applicant details on WhatsApp, SMS or personal email. Use comments in the sheet.

Pledge first

Email afrakomascholars@gmail.com confirming you will follow these rules.

Access ends

The Program Director removes your access once winners are paid and any thank-you messages have gone out.

Shared something by mistake? Tell the Program Director the same day. Do not try to fix it quietly.

If you know an applicant or their family, say so before you start. Another Desk member or the Program Director takes that application.

Your four steps

Work through them in order. Each one hands a cleaner list to the next.

  1. Intake screening

    Turn the raw responses into a clean list of unique, complete, eligible applications. Finish this before opening any documents.

    How to do it
    • Copy the response sheet as 2026 Desk Review and work only there.
    • Sort by BECE Index Number. Matching duplicates: keep the earliest complete one, mark the rest Duplicate. Conflicting duplicates: mark all Integrity review and settle them in step 2.
    • Flag the same MoMo number or guardian across different index numbers with a comment. Siblings can share a guardian.
    • Mark every row Eligible or Not eligible against the master handbook, with a one-line reason.

  2. Document verification

    Open each upload in Drive and compare it with the form, field by field.

    What to check
    DocumentMust showMust match in the form
    BECE Placement LetterStudent name, BECE Index Number, placed school, programmeFull name, BECE Index Number, Assigned SHS, Course of Study
    Form 1 Admission LetterStudent name, school name, 2026 admissionFull name, Assigned SHS
    Parent or Guardian Ghana CardFull name, clear photoGuardian name in Section 1

    Verified

    Every field matches and every document is clear.

    Query

    A small, likely innocent mismatch. Write the exact question; the Program Director settles it before selection.

    Failed

    Missing, unreadable, someone else's, or edited. Write the reason.

    Record the guardian's name exactly as printed on the Ghana Card. Callers need it for the MoMo check. Never copy the card number.

  3. Blind Applicant IDs and the Panel Sheet

    Strip out everything that could identify a student before the Panel sees anything.

    How to build it
    • Shuffle the verified rows with a helper column using =RAND(), then number them AFK26-001, AFK26-002 and so on.
    • The Applicant ID column in your Desk sheet is the ID key. It never leaves the Desk.
    • Fill the 2026 Panel Sheet (already set up, linked from Your tasks): Applicant ID, BECE Aggregate, Prompt 1 (redacted), Prompt 2 (redacted).
    • Replace names, schools, towns and phone numbers with [NAME], [SCHOOL], [TOWN]. Change nothing else.
    • A second Desk member checks every redaction, then ticks Ready on that row. Panelists see a row on the site only once it is Ready. Never share the sheet itself with them.

  4. Re-identify winners and hand off

    Fill the 2026 Winner Call Sheet (already set up, linked from Your tasks) with every verified student. Callers see a family on the site only once the Program Director marks its Applicant ID Selected.

    What goes on the Call Sheet
    ColumnContent
    Applicant IDSame ID as on the Panel Sheet
    Student full nameAs on the Placement Letter
    Guardian nameExactly as on the Ghana Card
    Guardian phone and MoMo numberFrom the form
    School and RegionAssigned SHS and its region
    Assigned caller emailOptional: leave blank so any caller can call
    Everything to the rightLeft blank: callers fill it in on the site

    Callers never see uploads, Ghana Card numbers, results, prompts or scores. If the Program Director chooses to thank applicants who were not selected, send a short, warm SMS or email once all winners are paid. Never mention scores or ranking.
