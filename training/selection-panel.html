Your stage is in burgundy: scoring.

What you see, and what you don't

The Verification Desk has already confirmed every application is genuine and eligible. You judge need and merit alone.

You see

An Applicant ID, the BECE aggregate, and two redacted answers: financial need and pay it forward.

You never see

Names, schools, towns, phone numbers or documents. You never learn which ID is which student.

If you recognise someone

Stop scoring that application and tell the Program Director, who will reassign it.

Score an application

Pick one score per criterion. The total updates as you go. Copy the three scores and the total into the Panel Sheet with a one-line note for each.

The rubric at a glance

The same scale for every applicant. Aggregate bands may be adjusted by the Program Director once the pool is known.

Financial need (45)

  • 5 Cannot cover entry costs without help, with clear specifics
  • 4 Serious strain, clearly described; can cover only a small part
  • 3 Real hardship; can cover part of the costs
  • 2 A general statement of need with few specifics
  • 1 No financial need described

Academic merit (35)

  • 5 Aggregate 6 to 10
  • 4 11 to 15
  • 3 16 to 20
  • 2 21 to 25
  • 1 26 or higher

Pay it forward (20)

  • 5 A specific, realistic plan tied to school, family or community
  • 4 A clear intention with some specifics
  • 3 A sincere but general intention
  • 2 A vague or borrowed answer
  • 1 Blank or off topic

How we score fairly

Substance, not English

Short, plain or imperfect writing is never marked down.

Stories before numbers

Score both prompts first, then look at the aggregate.

Score independently

With two Panel members, no discussion until both finish. Average the totals; talk through any gap over 15 points.

Keep it in the sheet

No prompt text in messages or chats. If anyone contacts you about an applicant, tell the Program Director.

Ranking and tie-breaks

Sort by final total. The top 5 IDs are recommended. The next highest, up to 3, become Reserves in order. In 2026, with 7 applicants, that means Reserve 1 and Reserve 2.

  1. Higher Financial need score

  2. Higher Pay it forward score

  3. Better (lower) BECE aggregate

  4. Still level: the Program Director decides and writes down why

Send the Program Director the ranked IDs with totals and notes. Your work ends there.
