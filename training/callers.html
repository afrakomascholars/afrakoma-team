Protect every family from scams

A call saying "you have won money" can sound like a scam. These rules apply on every call.

Never ask for

A MoMo PIN, a code, a password, or the Ghana Card number.

Never ask for money

No fee, charge or "processing cost". We never charge families anything.

One number

Make every call from the same number all cycle, so families recognise it.

Let them check

Tell them they can confirm the call is real at afrakomascholars@gmail.com.

The winner call, step by step

About 10 minutes, or 15 with a testimonial. Speak naturally, in the family's language. Keep the Call Sheet open.

Step 1

Confirm who you are speaking to

"Good day. My name is [YOUR NAME], and I'm calling from Afrakoma Scholarships. May I speak with [GUARDIAN NAME], the parent or guardian of [STUDENT NAME]?"

If someone else answers, do not share the news. Ask when the guardian can be reached.

Step 2

Share the news

"I'm happy to tell you that [STUDENT NAME] has been selected for the Afrakoma Scholarships 2026 SHS Form 1 Stipend Grant. The award is GHS 700.00, to help with provisions, uniforms, books and boarding supplies."

Give them a moment. Then ask: "How do you expect this to help [STUDENT NAME] this term?" Write it under Expected impact.

Step 3

Confirm the school

"Is [STUDENT NAME] in Form 1 at [SCHOOL]?"

Step 4

Confirm the Mobile Money account

"We will send the grant to [MoMo NUMBER]. What is the full name this account is registered under?"

We only pay the number in the application. Write the name exactly as said. It must match the guardian name on the Call Sheet or the student's full name.

Step 5

Explain what happens next

"The grant will be sent to this number by Wednesday, October 7. Before we send it, we check that the registered name matches. We will never ask you for your PIN, a code, or any payment. If anyone does, it is not us."

Step 6

Ask about the announcement

"We would love to celebrate [STUDENT NAME] on our social media. With your permission, we would share their full name, school and region. If you prefer, we can share only their first name and region. Which would you like?"

Record Full or First name only. If they are unsure, record First name only.

Step 7

Share the commitment

"Our only request is that [STUDENT NAME] remembers to pay it forward, and one day helps someone else the way they have been helped."

Step 8

Offer the testimonial

"Would you be willing to share a few words about what this means to your family? It is completely optional and does not affect the grant."

If yes, start recording and ask them to confirm their name and that they agree to us using it. Student too only with the guardian's permission, on the recording.

Step 9

Close the call

"Is there a phone number or email where we can send a written confirmation? You can always write to afrakomascholars@gmail.com. Congratulations again."

What do I do if…

Pick what is happening. Every exception goes to the Program Director; never bend the rules to get a family paid.

The MoMo name is slightly different

Write down the exact registered name and mark MoMo: Check. The Program Director decides before any payment.

The MoMo name is someone else entirely

Stay calm and promise nothing: "Thank you. Our team will review this and get back to you." Mark MoMo: No match and tell the Program Director today. Do not take a new number.

They ask us to pay someone else

Explain that we can only pay the number in the application, registered to the guardian or the student. Mark MoMo: No match.

The person who answers is not the guardian

Do not share the news. Ask when the guardian can be reached and call back.

No one answers

Try three times over at least two days, at different times, and log each attempt. After the third miss, mark Unreachable and tell the Program Director.

The guardian declines the grant

Thank them warmly, mark Declined, and tell the Program Director.

The guardian thinks it is a scam

That is a good sign. Thank them, give them afrakomascholars@gmail.com to confirm, and offer to call back at a time that suits them.

Testimonials

Optional, always

Say plainly that the grant does not depend on it. Never press for hardship details.

Consent on tape

Once recording, ask them to confirm their name and that we may use it. Students only with the guardian's permission, also on tape.

Same day

Upload to the Testimonials 2026 folder as 2026-[FIRST NAME]-[SCHOOL]-testimonial, then delete it from your phone.

After the call

  1. Save the call record on Your tasks

    Call log, MoMo registered name, MoMo result (Match, Check, No match), announcement choice, testimonial, confirmation contact, outcome and expected impact.

  2. The Program Director approves payment

    Only for families marked Confirmed with MoMo result Match, and only to the number in the application.

  3. Check the name before sending

    Whoever sends reads the recipient name on the MoMo confirmation screen. If it differs from the Call Sheet, cancel and tell the Program Director.

  4. Log it in the financial tracker

    Transaction ID, date and GHS 700.00, with the receipt saved.

  5. Send the written confirmation

    Dear [GUARDIAN NAME], congratulations again to [STUDENT NAME] on being selected for the Afrakoma Scholarships 2026 SHS Form 1 Stipend Grant. GHS 700.00 has been sent to your Mobile Money number ending [LAST 3 DIGITS]. Afrakoma Scholarships will never ask you for a PIN, code or payment. Questions: afrakomascholars@gmail.com. Remember to pay it forward.

The December check-in

Thursday, December 17 or Friday, December 18, 2026, by call or WhatsApp from the Afrakoma number. Check the family's Expected impact first. We are checking in, not checking up.

Ask the guardian

  • What did the grant go toward?
  • In October you hoped it would [EXPECTED IMPACT]. How did that turn out?
  • How has [STUDENT NAME] settled in?
  • Has anything been harder than expected?
  • Have you seen them paying it forward?
  • May we share your words, with or without your name?

Ask the student

Only with the guardian's agreement. Never ask about grades.

  • Favourite part of your first term?
  • The hardest part?
  • Did you have what you needed when term started?
  • How have you paid it forward, or how will you?
  • Anything to say to the people who support Afrakoma Scholars?

Record answers in the 2026 Follow-up sheet the same day, then delete any WhatsApp chat. Report only what families actually said.
