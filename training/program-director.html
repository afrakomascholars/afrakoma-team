Three teams, separate keys

No single person sees everything. Send each person only their own page.

Verification Desk

Sees documents and identities. Builds the blind Panel Sheet and the Winner Call Sheet.

Open the Desk page

Selection Panel

Sees only Applicant IDs, aggregates and redacted answers. Scores and ranks blind.

Open the Panel page

Callers

See only the Winner Call Sheet. Notify winners, check MoMo names, follow up in December.

Open the Callers page

Nobody serves on both the Desk and the Panel in the same cycle, and everyone emails a confidentiality pledge before getting access.

Decisions only you make

  1. Queries from the Desk

    Settle every Query before the Panel Sheet is shared. Nothing is left for the winner call.

  2. The final list

    Approve 5 winners and the Reserves from the Panel's ranked Applicant IDs. Break any tie the order cannot settle, and write down why.

  3. MoMo name mismatches

    In this first cycle you decide each case, as Program Director. Payment only ever goes to the number in the application.

  4. Payments

    Send from the founder's GT Bank account, check the name on the confirmation screen, and log every transfer in the tracker.

  5. Thank-you messages

    Decide whether the Desk thanks applicants who were not selected.

  6. The announcement

    Approve the post once all five payments are logged.

What the public sees

We share

Each student as their guardian chose: full name, school and region, or first name and region. Listed alphabetically.

We never share

Scores, results, family circumstances, guardian details, or the names of applicants who were not selected.
